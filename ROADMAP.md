# MANĀR Roadmap

**MANĀR** (مَنَار) — *A Beacon for Learning*

## Phase 0 — Brand + foundation
- Brand identity, logo system, colour palette
- Next.js App Router project structure
- Typography (English + Arabic)
- Design language (arch, stars, geometric accents)

## Phase 1 — Introductory MVP (current)
- Word of the Day, Number of the Day, Sum of the Day
- English ↔ Arabic vocabulary (~50 words)
- Magnet-style spelling and letter tiles
- Visual mathematics (+ − × ÷)
- Local progress (XP, streak, mastery) via `manar:v1`
- No accounts

## Phase 2 — Spelling + reading expansion
- Longer phrases and sentence templates
- More missing-letter and reading activities
- Expanded vocabulary by category

## Phase 3 — Audio + pronunciation
- Recorded native audio (replace/augment Web Speech)
- Slow / syllable pronunciation
- Optional listen-and-repeat guidance (still no required recording)

## Phase 4 — Adaptive learning
- Smarter word selection from mastery + mistakes
- Difficulty adaptation within levels
- Letter of the Day architecture enabled in UI

## Phase 5 — Accounts + child profiles
- Optional parent/child accounts
- Multiple local profiles on one device

## Phase 6 — Parent dashboard
- Progress overview for caregivers
- Gentle weekly summaries

## Phase 7 — Cloud synchronization
- `CloudStorageService` implementing `StorageService`
- Cross-device progress sync

## Phase 8 — Additional languages
- Afrikaans, French, Spanish, etc.
- Shared activity engines with language packs

## Phase 9 — Advanced maths + reading
- Larger numbers, fractions intro
- Longer comprehension passages

## Phase 10 — Stories + educational games
- Short illustrated stories
- Light educational games (still short-session oriented)

---

## Intentionally excluded from the MVP

- Login / registration / OAuth
- Parent, child, or teacher accounts
- Cloud database and sync
- Payments, subscriptions, ads
- Social features and leaderboards
- Advanced AI tutoring
- Voice recording requirements
- Complex analytics dashboards
